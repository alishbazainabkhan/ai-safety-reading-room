# The AI Safety Reading Room

**An annotated library of 190 AI safety sources, arranged so you can read the field in order instead of drowning in it.**

**[Open the reading room →](https://aisafetyed-rjn8b2pr.manus.space)** · **[Browse every source on GitHub →](SOURCES.md)**


When I switched to AI safety, I had no guide to tell me what to read or skip. It was a flat list and late nights of going through three different papers at once. After reading an ungodly amount of papers, and with the help of Manus, this site came to be, so no one has to be as lost as I was when starting in AI safety.

---

## What's inside

The library has three layers, each built for a different kind of reader.

### 1. The roadmap: 8 rooms, 40 sources

A guided path for someone starting from zero. Each room holds five sources and a short note on why you are reading them at that point.

| Room | What it covers |
|---|---|
| 01 Problem and objectives | What alignment is trying to control, and why proxy objectives fail |
| 02 Training-time alignment | Preference learning, constitutional methods, truthfulness, goal misgeneralization |
| 03 Attacks and robustness | Jailbreaks and injection, read before trusting any guardrail |
| 04 Reasoning and interpretability | Why visible reasoning helps monitoring while remaining incomplete |
| 05 Monitoring, oversight, and control | Protocols that assume the model may be working against you |
| 06 Agents and multimodal systems | What changes once a model can act on the world |
| 07 Privacy and social harms | Memorization, PII, fairness and toxicity as safety problems |
| 08 Evaluation and governance | Connecting technical controls to standards and regulation |

### 3. Domain index: 12 domains, 88 sources

A practitioner's shelf. It covers the engineering side of building safety systems, from guardrail frameworks and jailbreak research to PII detection, compliance regimes and the infrastructure documentation you need when you actually ship something.

## How to use it

- **New to the field?** Walk the roadmap from Room 01 to Room 08.
- **Looking into one area?** Go straight to the matching topic guide and read the annotations first.
- **Building something?** Use the domain index as a reference shelf.
- **Looking for one paper?** Use the search bar at the top of the site, or search [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) here on GitHub.

## Verification

Every link in the library has been checked against the title it claims to be. [`source-verification.csv`](source-verification.csv) lists each source with what its link actually opens. If you find a link that has broken since, please open an issue.
